About 406 Nation

We are a family-owned business that has proudly called Belgrade home since 2007. With Jena hailing from Bozeman and our four kids attending local schools, we are deeply connected to the heart of our community.

"In 2023, we realized there was a need—locals had limited access to Panther Pride and Belgrade-themed merchandise outside of school events."

That's when 406 Nation was born. Opening in July of 2024, we set out to create a place where anyone could find high-quality, custom apparel that proudly represents our local pride.

From fun designs for family adventures to professional gear for businesses, we offer custom apparel, personalized hats, and tumblers for everyone. Our goal is to foster a spirit of unity and collaboration within our community.
